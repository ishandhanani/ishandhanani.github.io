Previously, I was the founder of Agora Labs - a startup that built ML tooling on top of decentralized/neo cloud providers. We were acquired in February 2024 by [brev.dev](https://brev.dev) which was acquired by NVIDIA in June 2024.

In my undergrad I studied economics and statistics. I had a brief stint at Columbia University before dropping out to work on brev.dev. Please reach out if you're interested in any of my work and my journey!
